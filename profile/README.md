## Trading, at the speed of conversation.

The market doesn't wait while you switch tabs. Ask TrabyOS for a price, bring up a chart,
check your positions, or prepare your next trade — in your own words.
Review the details on screen. Change your mind mid-flow. Give the final go-ahead.

**Your voice starts the trade. Your approval makes the call.**

<br />

## For traders. For trading platforms.

**For individuals — a voice-first trading assistant.** Use TrabyOS on the web or Mac to explore
markets, manage trading workflows, and review orders in natural language.

**For exchanges and partners — our voice module, inside your platform.** We work directly with
exchange platforms and partner companies to provide our voice technology within their own
trading experiences. The integration work brings market questions, order preparation, and explicit
user approval into the platform traders already use.

We are collaborating with multiple exchanges and partners on module delivery, development, and
ongoing operation, alongside our own web and Mac experiences.

**A trading assistant you can use. Voice technology your platform can offer.**

## Our vision: Any market. Anywhere. Just your voice.

**An AI assistant that makes trading crypto and stocks as natural as speaking — wherever you are.**

We envision one assistant across the systems and devices you use: from web and Mac to Windows,
Android, and iOS, and onward to earbuds, smartwatches, and smart glasses. Ask about the market,
review a trade, and give your approval by voice, without having to reach for a trading screen.

Web, Mac, and partner-platform integrations are our focus today. Broader device support and stock
trading are part of our long-term vision.

**Your markets. Your assistant. With you, wherever life takes you.**

<br />

## On the web. Built for your Mac.

[Open TrabyOS in your browser](https://www.trabyos.com/) and sign in with Google.

On Mac, a native workspace stays at the top of your screen. Speak when it's natural; type when you need quiet.
English and Korean input. The same review-and-approve experience in paper and live trading.

TrabyOS connects to your exchange. You stay in control of what gets placed.

<details>
<summary><strong>The technology behind the experience</strong></summary>
<br />

Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit for the native Mac experience. Python and OpenAI for AI and voice.
Java and Spring Boot for backend services. TypeScript and React for web experiences.
PostgreSQL and WebSockets for data and streaming.

TrabyOS is a proprietary product. This GitHub profile shares product information and selected visuals;
our product repositories and implementation remain private.
