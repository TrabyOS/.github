## Trading, at the speed of conversation.

The market doesn't wait while you switch tabs. Ask TrabyOS for a price, bring up a chart,
check your positions, or prepare your next trade — in your own words.
Review the details on screen. Change your mind mid-flow. Give the final go-ahead.

**Your voice starts the trade. Your approval makes the call.**

## Made for your Mac

A native workspace at the top of your screen. Speak when it's natural; type when you need quiet.
English and Korean input. The same review-and-approve experience in paper and live trading.

TrabyOS connects to your exchange. You stay in control of what gets placed.

<details>
<summary><strong>The technology behind the experience</strong></summary>
<br />

Swift, SwiftUI, and AppKit for the native Mac experience. Python and OpenAI for AI and voice.
Java and Spring Boot for backend services. TypeScript and React for web experiences.
PostgreSQL and WebSockets for data and streaming.

TrabyOS is a proprietary product. This GitHub profile shares product information and selected visuals;
our product repositories and implementation remain private.

</details>
