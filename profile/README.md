### The market moves fast. Just say the word.

TrabyOS is an AI trading platform that brings market research, account insights, and real trading
into a voice-driven workspace. Check a price. Pull up a chart. Review your positions. Prepare an order.
Speak naturally, see the details, and give the go-ahead — all from your Mac.

Connect your exchange account to trade live, or explore the same workflow with a paper account.

### From “What’s happening?” to “Place the trade.”

| Understand the market | See your account | Prepare your next move |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ask about prices, explore charts, and catch up on market headlines. | Bring balances, positions, open orders, and trading history into view. | Prepare a trade, review its details, and approve, revise, or cancel the proposal. |

**Speak or type.** Use voice when it feels natural, or type when you need quiet.
English and Korean input are supported.

**Review before acting.** Trade proposals appear as cards you can inspect and change.
An order requires your explicit approval.

**Real trading, on your terms.** Trade through your connected exchange account, including spot
and perpetual markets where supported. Practice with a simulated paper account whenever you want.
TrabyOS is the interface; your connected exchange is the trading venue.
